# Dice notation

| Notation | Means |
|---|---|
| `d20` | one twenty-sided die (same as `1d20`) |
| `2d6` | two six-sided dice, added together |
| `3d8+4` | three eight-sided dice, plus 4 |
| `4d6-1` | four six-sided dice, minus 1 |

Common dice: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, and d100 (percentile). Several rolls can be given at once:
`roll.py d20 2d6`.

Limits: 1–1000 dice, 2–1,000,000 sides. A modifier is a whole number.
